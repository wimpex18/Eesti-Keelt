## Notes on the rules this log applied

- Until the second revision batch, `draft.py` sent back a draft whose gates
  dropped any question, even with five left, and every text with fewer than
  five removable endings for reading task 4; such drafts were copied to
  `rejected/`. From then on a draft goes back only when it must (a gate's
  failure, fewer than five questions, a digit in an answer, fewer than the four
  endings `harnotasks.phrase_bank` needs), because pushing an A1 text past
  four made the model bolt on clauses (*lill, mis on punane*). The three drafts
  that were copied to `rejected/` and later accepted unchanged
  (`eile/puhkepaev` r0, `kodu/uus-korter` r0, `kohvik/lounapaus` r2) were
  removed from it.
- A *scratch run* blind-checks every question the gates kept, outside the
  repository, to learn which survive; `draft.py trim` then keeps five of
  those, and the *recorded run* writes `checked/`. A question can pass the
  scratch run and fail the recorded one; that file was trimmed again from its
  spare survivors, or revised, and checked again.
