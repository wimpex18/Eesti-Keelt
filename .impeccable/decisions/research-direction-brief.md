# Grove replacement direction brief

Mode: Operate. Workflow: code-first. Scope: the entire learning application.
Research: docs/learning-product-research.md. Seed: 6ecca7d2, reroll 1,
assigned grounded index 6. Practice rhythm is approved by the user; the six-block contract is recorded.

The learner studies Estonian between ordinary activities on a phone and does
longer reading/writing at a desk. Russian is the MVP explanation language;
English/Ukrainian content architecture is prepared without claiming full coverage.
The unique mechanism joins source-backed learning, checked practice and a clear
next step, with reversible navigation skips independent of mastery.

The home must prove that a learner can start/resume without interpreting a
dashboard. All four skills remain reachable, sessions have an obvious return,
and course/review/exam/account utilities have one predictable home each.
Existing progress, source identities and working grading survive consolidation.
Colours, fonts, glass, boardwalk, layouts and the three-mode shell are replaceable.

The category rut is a gamified path surrounded by streaks, rewards and catalogue
tiles; its predictable opposite is a bare monochrome dashboard. Neither is in
the grounded list. Prior Study desk, Learning library, Chapter tabs and the prior
catalog hand are excluded from this new round.

## Fresh grounded candidates, ordered by audience resonance

1. **Everyday phrases** — adult bilingual phrase-card graphics: plum navigation,
   lilac ground, useful sentence-sized titles and plain tasks; a phrase expands
   from example to practice. The home leads with a useful lesson and four skills.
2. **Welcome** — newcomer civic welcome graphics: ink-blue, clear sky and orange;
   short purpose statements, one action and an in-context help reveal. Suitable
   for learners who need to understand what is expected before answering.
3. **Conversation captions** — bilingual subtitle design: graphite and cyan,
   comfortable humanist sans, a transcript line expands to replay/response.
   Skill navigation remains standard and text stays readable during recording.
4. **Language weekend** — Baltic cultural-programme typography: violet and lime,
   concise headings, an explicit session sequence that continues one step at a
   time. Its energy transfers to the learning shell without poster composition.
5. **Clear passage** — Estonian plain-language public-information graphics:
   deep teal and pale blue, generous reading measures, term explanation on
   demand. Reading and writing gain the same task-first hierarchy as drills.
6. **Practice rhythm** — Baltic woven colour bands: spruce navigation, mint
   working ground and coral current-step marks; a labelled learn/practise/check
   strip advances after action. No textile ornament, physical loom controls or
   novel navigation. Standard components retain familiar behaviour.
7. **Picture and phrase** — adult illustrated phrasebook graphics: indigo and
   apricot, clear sans, an example/media caption opens its associated practice.
   Authored educational imagery is used only when it contributes to learning.

Each world contributes type, palette, density and one signature interaction;
the product task defines navigation and layout. Candidate 6 is the assigned
direction and candidate 1 is the single pick. No aesthetic reroll is inferred
from the owner's broad redesign permission.

## Common product commitments

- Three entry routes; start earlier remains available after an assessment.
- Home offers one start/resume action, with course/review controls secondary.
- Phone has four labelled skill destinations and a visible home return;
  desktop has one compact navigation area, without a repeated mode switch.
- One task at a time; explanation/example, practice, feedback, next.
- Topic/activity/word skips are reversible and distinct from checked test-out.
- Useful default skill activities; one recovery action for missing content,
  microphone denial, offline use and provider failures.
- Course, Review/Vocabulary, Exam and Account/Progress consolidate the current
  eleven-tab structure without discarding progress or hiding common tasks deeply.
- No exam date or readiness dashboard until relevant to a chosen goal.

## Challenger judgement

| Dealt world | Audience identification | Product clarity after fusion | Verdict / discipline retained |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monochrome product proof | Adult software familiarity, little connection to learning | A lesson paired with its answer is clear, but repeated proof panels compete with the task | Declined; each result shows the evidence actually checked |
| Goldenrod timetable slides | Departure notation is remote from this audience's study scene | State cells can read well; raked surfaces and lateral travel complicate long text | Declined; stable positions for controls and clear state labels |
| Vertical media stage | Familiar short-media focus and caption reading | One activity dominates, but course orientation and long writing need extra care | Competitive; full alternate, with a next-item transition confined to completed activities |
| Saville catalogue | Cultural cover design is recognisable, coded labels are remote from novice learning | The active lesson can be readable; encoded names and hover decoding obscure destinations | Declined; one decisive task title rather than several competing summaries |
| Midnight transit graphics | Route-following is familiar but learning is not a transit task | The four-skill relationships can be clear; a network competes with simple next lesson | Declined; keep a visible return to the current learning place |
| Split-flap concourse | Familiar in travel, weak connection to reflective learning | Fixed columns help status; cascading letter animation slows scanning | Declined; reserve state space so feedback does not move controls |

The assigned direction's named raises must be visible in the chooser. The media
world remains a standard working app with familiar buttons, tabs and text fields;
it cannot become an endless video feed or gesture-only learning flow.
